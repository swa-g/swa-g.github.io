# People photos

Put a photo here named after the person: first and last name in lower case, joined with a hyphen, e.g.

    nibaran-das.jpg
    teresa-goncalves.jpg      (no accents)
    branko-ster.jpg

Square or near-square pictures at about 200x200 px or larger work best (they show as a small round thumbnail).
Then regenerate `assets/people.js` (or ask Claude to) and the photo appears. People without a photo show their initials.
Only add photos you have permission to publish.
