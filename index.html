About

Human - Artist - Researcher

I'm a postdoctoral fellow at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science, University of Ljubljana, funded by a Marie Skłodowska-Curie COFUND fellowship. My project, LANCER, studies how a reinforcement-learning agent can learn to navigate complex driving environments safely.

My path here started in Kolkata, where I completed my PhD in Engineering at Jadavpur University in 2022, co-supervised by a colleague at the University of Évora in Portugal, where I was also an Erasmus Mundus fellow. I then spent three years as an Associate Professor at the Institute of Engineering & Management, where I founded a Centre of Excellence for Data Science and ran the AI & ML programme.

Earlier work includes compact segmentation networks for driving scenes, cell-image classification for medical imaging, and graph-based models for text and image captions.

Open to collaboration. If you work on autonomous systems, medical imaging or document understanding and think we should talk, please get in touch.

Projects

Current project: LANCER

Ongoing · Nov 2025 – Nov 2027 · MSCA COFUND (SMASH)

LANCER

Learning Autonomous Navigation in Complex Environment using Reinforcement

A postdoctoral project at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science, University of Ljubljana. It studies how reinforcement-learning agents can learn to drive safely in complex environments, using a CARLA-based sensor pipeline (RGB, depth, semantic segmentation and LiDAR across configurable weather).
